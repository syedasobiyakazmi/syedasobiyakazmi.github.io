Frequently asked questions

Do you only work with aesthetic clinics?

What does the free SEO audit include?
A short review of your website, your Google Business Profile, how you appear for your main treatments, and what your top local competitors are doing better. You get it in writing, and there’s no obligation to buy anything afterwards.
What are GEO and AEO?
GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) is about getting your clinic mentioned and cited in AI-generated answers when patients ask AI tools for a recommendation. AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) is about winning the direct answer at the top of Google, featured snippets and “People also ask”. Neither can be guaranteed, but content can be structured to compete for both.
Are you an expert in semantic SEO?
I’m currently learning semantic SEO and applying it in my work, topical maps, central entity and semantic content briefs, following Koray Tuğberk Gübür’s framework. So far I’ve delivered 500+ briefs for clinic websites, 100+ briefs for an accounting website and 5+ topical maps. My 3 years of experience are in clinic SEO overall; semantic SEO is the specialism I’m actively deepening, not a certification I claim.
What is a topical map?
A topical map is the full plan of everything your clinic needs to publish to own a topic, the central entity, every treatment (core section), and every supporting sub-topic, attribute and patient question (outer section), in the right order. You build the map first, then write, the opposite of picking random keywords month by month.
What is a semantic content brief?
It’s the writing plan for one page, built from the topical map: the central entity, the attributes and facts it must cover, the search intent, the H1–H3 outline, the questions it must answer, and the internal links in and out. It makes sure every page covers the topic properly and connects to the rest of the map.
How long before SEO shows results?
Foundations and your Google profile can improve visibility within the first few weeks. Meaningful ranking and enquiry growth usually builds over 3–6 months of consistent work. Anyone promising page one in a week is not being honest with you.
Do I need to sign a long contract?
No. Packages run month to month. The monthly report shows exactly what was done and what it produced, so you can judge the value yourself and stop if it isn’t working.
Why not just hire a big agency?
With an agency, your clinic is one of many accounts. With me, you deal directly with the person doing the work, at a freelancer’s price, with clinic-specific experience, and a monthly report that shows the work, not just a summary.
